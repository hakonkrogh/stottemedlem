# Concept: Brand mark (the heart)

**Status:** Active

## Definition
The product's visual mark is a **red heart** — the ordinary red heart character
(❤️), not a custom-drawn icon. It is the favicon of every app, and it rides
along wherever the "støttemedlem.no" wordmark appears.

## Why it exists
The product is about people choosing to back an organization they care about —
a supporting membership is an act of affection, not a purchase. The heart says
that in one glyph, and gives the product something instantly recognizable in a
browser tab, an email footer, or the corner of a printed card. Using the plain
character keeps it universal: it renders everywhere, needs no asset pipeline,
and never competes with an organization's own identity.

## Rules & invariants
- **The heart is the favicon** of every app (public pages, back office,
  marketing site).
- **The heart accompanies the wordmark**: wherever
  [brand attribution](brand-attribution.md) puts "støttemedlem.no" in visible
  text, the heart sits in front of it ("❤️ støttemedlem.no").
- **It is the standard red heart character** — no custom icon, no other color.
  Where an SVG surface cannot rely on emoji fonts, a simple red heart shape may
  stand in, but the character is the default.
- The heart is a mark, not a headline: like the attribution it belongs to, it
  stays small and quiet next to the organization's own identity.
- The heart means **supporting**: the product carries one as its mark, and a
  member collects one per supported year on their
  [scorecard](scorecard.md). The shared glyph is deliberate — every heart a
  member has earned is the brand, restated.

## Relationships
- Extends [brand attribution](brand-attribution.md) — the heart travels with
  the wordmark that attribution requires.
- The [scorecard](scorecard.md)'s hearts are the same glyph, earned one per
  supported year.
- The heart is the one red in the [brand palette](brand-palette.md); no
  button, label or highlight shares its colour.
