# Problem: Loyalty is invisible and word of mouth goes untracked

**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Small organizations and their long-standing supporters

## Who has this problem
Supporting members who have backed an organization for years, and the
organizations that depend on word of mouth — in practice their only marketing
channel — to grow the member list.

## The problem
Years of support leave no visible trace: the member who has paid loyally for a
decade is indistinguishable from someone who joined last week, so there is
nothing to feel proud of and nothing for the organization to celebrate. At the
same time, recruiting happens person-to-person — a member talks a friend into
joining — but nothing captures that the join came from that member. The
organization cannot see or thank its best recruiters, and the member gets no
recognition for growing the community they care about.

## Why it matters
Recognition is a big part of why people keep supporting a small organization —
invisible loyalty lapses more easily. And untracked word of mouth means the
strongest growth channel these organizations have cannot be encouraged,
thanked, or repeated deliberately.

## What "solved" looks like
- A member can see and show off how many years they have supported the
  organization and how many members they have brought in.
- A member can hand someone a scannable way to join — on a screen, in a chat,
  or printed — that credits the join back to them.
- The organization can see who its most loyal members and best recruiters are,
  and thank them.
