# Problem: Supporters never hear back from the organization

**Status:** Draft
**Owner:** Small organizations (marching bands, choirs, sports teams, community groups)

## Who has this problem
Organizations with supporting members, and the supporters themselves. A
supporting member pays a yearly fee out of goodwill — and then, typically,
hears nothing until (at best) next year's payment request.

## The problem
Small organizations have no easy way to stay in touch with the people who
support them. Contact details live in scattered spreadsheets (when they were
captured at all), so even a simple thank-you is a chore. Supporters who never
hear back feel like a bank account rather than a part of the community; the
goodwill that made them join fades, and next year's renewal quietly doesn't
happen. Organizations know gratitude and involvement drive renewal, but the
tooling makes silence the default.

## Why it matters
For the supporter, the whole point of supporting is feeling connected to the
organization — silence breaks that. For the organization, unthanked and
uninformed supporters lapse; keeping a member is far cheaper than finding a
new one. For the product, a register that only collects payment misses half
the promise: the member list is only valuable if the organization can act on
it.

## What "solved" looks like
- The organization can reach its supporting members (thanks, updates, news)
  without exporting lists or maintaining a separate contact register.
- A supporter hears from the organization they support at least when it
  matters: on joining, and when the organization has something to share.
- Communication uses the same always-current register that payments maintain —
  no second source of truth.
